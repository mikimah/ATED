Conference Overview

In the context of rapid advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI), which has become a core driver of global innovation, the connection of knowledge, enhancement of interdisciplinary collaboration, and promotion of AI applications in practice are increasingly essential. Under the theme “Shaping the Future with AI: Innovation, Integration and Impact,”. ,” the conference aims to establish an academic and practical forum for sharing cutting-edge research, fostering technology transfer, and generating positive societal impacts. Saigon Technology University (STU), in collaboration with domestic and international partners, is pleased to organize the International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Technology, Engineering and Design, scheduled to take place from October 29 to October 31, 2026. The conference will bring together scientists, experts, and practitioners to exchange academic knowledge, share practical experiences, and promote collaboration in the fields of artificial intelligence, technology, engineering, and design. We cordially invite scholars, experts, and enterprises to submit papers and participate in the conference, contributing to shaping the future with AI towards sustainable development and meaningful societal values.

WRITING GUIDE

You must apply to the Congress by sending an abstract. Since our congress has an INTERNATIONAL character, it is obligatory to send an abstract in English. The summary evaluation process takes a maximum of 5 days. The author is informed about the result of the evaluation without delay.

Abstract Writing Rules

Margins: 2.5 cm

Font: Times New Roman, 12 Points

Line Spacing: 1.00 cm

TITLE: All capital letters

Keywords: At least 3

Number of Words: At least 250

Your abstracts should be between 200-300 words. In addition, all authors in the paper must have their name, surname, place of work, the field of study, e-mail addresses, mobile phone numbers, and ORCID numbers.

You can submit your abstracts in word/doc/docs format until October, 2026 at the latest ATED@stu.edu.vn should be sent to the address.

(Abstract Sample Format)

If you want to have your full texts published in the Congress book, after your abstract is accepted, you can submit your FULL TEXTS until October, 2026 at 17:00 to our address ATED@stu.edu.vn. Studies with a similarity rate above 20% in full texts are not taken into consideration.

Full-Text Writing Rules

Margins: 2.5 cm

Font: Times New Roman, 12 Points

Line Spacing: 1.00 cm

TITLE: All capital letters

Subtitle: Small

The number of pages: Must be at least 5 pages. The upper limit is 25 pages

In your full texts, SUMMARY, INTRODUCTION, MATERIALS AND METHODS, FINDINGS and DISCUSSION, CONCLUSION and RECOMMENDATIONS, REFERENCES should be written under separate headings. Citation Format: APA 6 Style citation format should be used. In addition, all authors in the paper must have their name, surname, place of work, the field of study, city, and country, e-mail addresses, mobile phone numbers, and mandatory ORCID numbers
